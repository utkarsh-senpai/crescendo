How to play

It's like fantasy sports — for music

  1. Pick a genre — Pop, EDM or Bollywood. You get a budget of $100. More popular artists cost more, leaving less room for bold picks.
  2. Choose 5 artists you think will grow the most over the next 30 days. The number on each card is their predicted growth score — bigger = growing faster.
  3. Lock your team. An AI then builds its own team and shows you every pick it made and why — no hidden moves.
  4. See who was right. The team with the highest average real growth wins. Big subscriber counts don't help — only genuine acceleration does. ⚠ Inorganic = growth that looks artificially inflated, and it gets penalised.
Real artists, demo predictions

The artists and their current subscriber counts are real (pulled live from YouTube). The growth predictions are demo estimates for now — a tracker is building up real history daily, and once it has enough data the predictions will be based on real trends.

How scoring works

Growth, not fame

  1. The number on each card is a prediction of how much that artist will grow over the next 30 days. It's based on recent subscriber and view trends — not on how big they already are.
  2. Your score = the average actual growth across your 5 picks at the end of the window. Picking one massive artist doesn't help — all 5 picks count equally.
  3. Artificially inflated growth (⚠ Inorganic) doesn't count. If an artist's numbers look like they were boosted by bots or paid promotion, that growth is discounted.
  4. You vs the AI: the AI builds its own team under the same budget and explains every choice. Higher average growth wins. The AI isn't hiding anything.
Where the numbers come from
Now — demo mode

Subscriber and view counts are live from YouTube. The growth predictions driving scores are realistic demo estimates — not yet based on real tracked history.

Coming — real data

A tracker is recording real YouTube stats for every artist every day. Once it has built up enough history (a few weeks), scores will switch to predictions based on what actually happened. No changes needed on your end.
