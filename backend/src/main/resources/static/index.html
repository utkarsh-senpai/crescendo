How to play

Spot the next breakout

  1. Draft 5 emerging artists under a $100 salary cap. Cheaper artists leave room for more upside.
  2. The board is scored live by the /predict model — momentum, not hype. Artists whose growth looks bought are flagged ⚠ Inorganic and discounted.
  3. Lock your roster and a transparent AI drafts against you — showing every pick, its value-per-$, and every artist it passed on and why.
  4. Score the season on realised organic growth. Beat the AI, climb the leaderboard.
